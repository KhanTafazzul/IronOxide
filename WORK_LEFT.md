## 🏆 Top 5 Architectural Blockers for 100% Playability & Vanilla Parity

### 1. Active Redstone Simulation Engine & Scheduled Tick Queue (`net.minecraft.world.ticks.LevelTicks`)
* **The Gap**: World generation now successfully places complex redstone traps (Desert Pyramid 9-TNT pressure plate, Jungle Temple 3-lever piston puzzle and dispenser tripwires, Ancient City comparator lab). However, without an active redstone signal propagation loop in `src/world/`, wires do not emit signals, repeaters do not delay, comparators do not measure containers, and pistons do not push/pull blocks.
* **Requirements**:
  - Implement a 20Hz block tick priority queue (`LevelTicks`) with tick delays (1 to 4 ticks for repeaters).
  - Implement 15-to-0 power attenuation and canonical directional update order (`WEST`, `EAST`, `DOWN`, `UP`, `NORTH`, `SOUTH`).
  - Implement quasi-connectivity (QC) for pistons, dispensers, and droppers.

### 2. Comprehensive Tile Entity Runtime & Persistence Engine (`net.minecraft.world.level.block.entity.*`)
* **The Gap**: Currently, only `ChestTileEntity` is stored in chunk sections and serialized to `.mca` Anvil files. Workstations like furnaces and brewing stands exist only as detached structs in `mc_gameplay`.
* **Requirements**:
  - Unify `Furnace`, `BlastFurnace`, `Smoker`, `BrewingStand`, `Hopper`, `Dispenser`, `SculkShrieker`, `TrialSpawner`, and `CreakingHeart` into `TileEntity` in `src/world/tile_entity.rs`.
  - Wire Anvil NBT serialization for all tile entities (`BurnTime`, `CookTime`, `Items`, `warning_level`).
  - Implement Hopper item transfer logic (5-slot storage, pulling from container above, pushing to container facing, 8-tick transfer cooldown).

### 3. Boss Fight State Machines & Specialized Hostile Mob Behaviors (`net.minecraft.world.entity.boss.*`)
* **The Gap**: While regular hostiles, Illagers, and 1.21 mobs (Warden, Breeze, Creaking) are implemented, the iconic Minecraft climax encounters are missing:
* **Requirements**:
  - **Ender Dragon**: Phase manager (`EnderDragonPhaseManager`) executing `HoldingPattern`, `StrafePlayer`, `LandingApproach`, `PerchAndRoar`, `BreathAttack`, and `DyingAnimation`; End Crystals healing beam connection; dragon breath particle clouds.
  - **Wither Boss**: 3-head independent targeting, invulnerability spawn phase, Wither skull projectile barrage, and 50% HP armored state.
  - **Enderman**: Provocation logic (crosshair looking at head within 64m), instant teleportation on arrow projectile approach or water contact.
  - **Shulker**: Peek/close shell armor defense (80% damage reduction when closed) and homing levitation bullets.

### 4. Experience (XP), Enchanting Table & Potion Brewing Systems
* **The Gap**: Survival progression currently stops at workstation crafting. Tools cannot be enchanted, potions cannot be brewed, and XP is not awarded.
* **Requirements**:
  - **XP System**: Physical `XpOrb` entity with bobbing/magnetism, standard level threshold formula ($7 + 2 \times \text{level}$), XP drops from mining ore and slaying mobs.
  - **Enchanting Table**: 15-bookshelf scanning radius, pseudo-random seed generation for 3 selectable enchantments, lapis lazuli requirement, and applying vanilla enchantments (Sharpness, Protection, Efficiency, Fortune, Silk Touch, Unbreaking, Mending).
  - **Brewing Stand**: Blaze powder fuel bar (20 brews per powder), 3-bottle base slots, Nether Wart awkward potion conversion, and ingredient reaction chain.
  - **Status Effects**: `MobEffect` runtime attached to living entities with duration/amplifier ticking (Poison, Regeneration, Strength, Speed, Haste, Mining Fatigue, Night Vision, Darkness, Bad Omen).

### 5. Client Dynamic Asset Animation, Particle Systems & Full Positional Audio
* **The Gap**: The client graphics and sound engine currently lack several visual and atmospheric elements of the vanilla game:
* **Requirements**:
  - **Animated Textures**: Cycling UV sprite sheet frames for Water (32 frames), Lava (20 frames), Fire (32 frames), and Nether Portal (32 frames) running at 20Hz on the GPU.
  - **Ambient Particle Engine**: 3D billboarded sprite particles for torch flames/smoke, campfire plumes, nether portal swirls, spore blossoms, critical hit sparks, sculk souls, and potion swirls.
  - **Complete Soundscape**: Mob sounds (groans, rattles, hisses, steps, hurt/death screams), arrow shooting/hitting sounds, explosions, chest/door hinges, 19 ambient cave noises triggered in dark subterranean pockets, and background music player with randomized cooldown intervals.
  - **Entity Visual Polish**: 3D held items in mob/player hands, armor layer overlays with smithing trims, mob death 90-degree tipping animation, and ground shadow decals.
